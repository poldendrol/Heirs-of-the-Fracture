EPISODE I

THE KEEPER OF THE HEART

Long ago the EMPYREALS ruled the multiverse from NEXUSAR, the Nexus of Realms. They made the races of Vellorum to serve them, and they forged a primordial dragon to enforce their perfect order.

Then a spark of imagination struck the dragon. It became the LOREWYRM, possibility given form, and the empire of perfect order shattered.

Ages later, on the floating islands of DRIFTHOLM, a forgotten family of adventurers hears a warm voice from an old Waystone. It calls itself the KEEPER.

“Seven lords of Vellorum carry a flaw,” it says. “A sickness in the weave of the worlds. Cut it out of them and bring it to me, and everything will heal.”

Every heir will fall, and every fall makes the next heir stronger…

No one in the family asks why the Keeper never shows its face.

    HOW TO PLAY

    • Move AD or arrows · Jump Space or K, again in the air · Dash Shift in any direction you hold, also in the air, and you dodge through attacks while dashing
    • Light attack J · Heavy I · Bow hold L to aim, release to fire · Block hold Q, tap it as a hit lands to parry. Hold J while moving to sprint. Hold W to attack up, hold S in the air to attack down or fall faster. See Moves & combos on the title screen.
    • Ability F costs mana. Hits and kills refill mana.
    • Talk / open / buy E · Map Tab or M · Drop through platforms S + jump · Pause Esc
    • Slide down walls and jump off them. Controllers work too (PS5 shown): Cross jump, Square light attack (hold while moving to sprint), Triangle heavy, Circle bow (hold to aim in any direction with either stick, release to fire), R2 dash in any direction, R1 ability, L1 block and parry, d-pad up interact and revive, Options pause, Create or touchpad map. In menus: d-pad moves, Cross picks, Circle goes back.
    • Every heir is different. When yours dies, pick one of their children and spend the family gold in the Hidden Dojo, at the Blacksmith and with the Enchantress.
    • Old Murk takes all your remaining gold as the toll to the Waystones. Spend it before you go.
    • Each country ends in a boss. Beat it to open the road to the next country. Old Murk will row you to any border you have opened.
    • Co-op: up to 4 players. Everyone keeps their own gold on their own account. Stand on a fallen friend's grave and hold E or up to raise them.

    MOVES & COMBOS

    THE BASICS

    J □Light attack. Press three times for a 3-hit chain; the third hit lunges. Hold it while moving to sprint. I △Heavy attack. A slow wind-up, then a huge hit that knocks enemies flying. L ○Bow. Hold to aim (you stand still). On a controller you can aim in any direction with either stick, release to fire. A longer draw shoots harder and pierces. Your quiver is small: arrows stick in walls and enemies, so go and pick them up. One regrows slowly. Q L1Block and parry. Hold to block hits from the front. Tap it right as a hit lands to parry: no damage, nearby enemies are stunned, shots fly back, and your next hit is a guaranteed crit. Shift R2Dash. In any of 8 directions, also in the air. You dodge through attacks while dashing. F R1Class ability. Costs mana. Hits and kills refill it.

    COMBOS

Pops an enemy into the air and hops you up after it. □ in the airJuggle. Light hits keep an airborne enemy floating. ↓ + △ in the airGround slam.
